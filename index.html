How grades are worked out

Grades and bands use the average of the 2025 and 2026 National 5 D&M grade boundaries: A from 70.3%, B from 60.0%, C from 49.7%, D from 39.4%. Within each grade, the top half is the higher band: A1 from 85.2%, B3 from 65.2%, C5 from 54.9%. They’re a guide: real boundaries cover the whole course, coursework included. In the final exam the question paper is worth 80 marks: 30 for workshop manufacture (always question 1), 30 for design and 20 for commercial manufacture. Your weakest area is where to start.

5Exam technique

The course reports say the same things every year. These rules apply to every question.

Name, state
A word or short phrase is enough. Be exact: “outside callipers”, not “callipers”.
Describe
Give the steps or features in sentences. One clear point for each mark.
Explain
Give a reason and its consequence: “wax is applied with a cloth, so there is no brush to clean”.
Never
“Quick”, “cheap” or “easy” on their own score zero. Always say why or compared with what.
Read the stem
Don’t repeat what the question already tells you (“pine” when it says red pine). Check if it asks about a designer or a manufacturer, and a hand tool or a machine.
